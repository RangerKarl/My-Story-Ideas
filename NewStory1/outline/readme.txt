General outline: Macross mercenary work as gig economy.

A quiet, empty air conditioned guild hall. Automated smart teller machines are arranged in neat rows at the front. A human/zent guideperson is seated at the back. She is bored. Since the transition to online registry, people take their gig contracts and rewards/recriminations through the Internet, through couriers, through a network of third party service providers each shilling anything a freelancer would want.

The air changes. A sudden outgoing gust as the automated doors shift a mite too fast. [i] Are the smart building systems acting up again?[/i] she thinks as a young man all but bullrushes in.

He takes in the scene like a thirsty man takes in a bottle of water. Eyes casting about, his brown eyes framed in unkempt hair catch onto the guide...and slide off to a teller machine.
