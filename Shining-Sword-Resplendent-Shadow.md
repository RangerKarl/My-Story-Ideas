# Shining Sword, Resplendent Shadow

*a world of macross sidestory*

**by RangerKarl**


## Chapter 1

The apparent sky above Mikael’s head grumbled, the reddening glow deepening behind the image of heavy clouds.“Rain.” He looked at his weekly groceries in his crooked arm. A large paper bag held his goods, miscellaneous foodstuffs, and a small can of food for Greymalkin. There was no way, Mikael pondered, that the corner store would have bothered to shell out for the triple-sheet reinforced bags. If he was a significant distance away from his quarters, it would have been an issue, but thankfully, he was already on the threshold of his flat’s entrance.

The squat, 5-storey building had a pristine signboard. “Greenvale Garden”. The name was the only thing remotely green about it. A facade that would have been called a Soviet throwback, were it not for the hyper-modern trash cans and the e-ink public billboard on one of the walls in the general corridor.

Mikael walked up to the elevator leading up to his floor, like he did every day while on layover. The door on Elevator 4 drew open with a ring of a bell. He got in and the elevator automatically selected the floor for him, inferred by biometrics and his registration with the City’s offices. A plodding 2 minute ascent (easily sprinted up, or down, if needed) brought him up, and with another 30 second stride, he was home.

“Meowwww…”, said the furry creature sitting in the hallway of the apartment.

“Alright Grey, I’ve got it. Do not fret.”

Outside, the simulated weather started its loop. A flash, followed 0.35 seconds later by a rolling rumble to evoke a distant storm. The wind started to pick up outside.

Mikael’s phone was on his counter, along with some documents nested in polymer files. On the phone, several notifications. Simple emails, from automated services. Mikael ignored the phone, putting his paper bag down on the same counter. Rummaging in the bag he took out the can of cat food

The dwelling was spartan, even by the standards of Spartans. A potted plant in the corner and a 2 seat sofa (because one of his flight mates wanted to change furniture) dominated one side of the living room. A cheap flat screen sitting on a stack of old manuals, the other.
