Stepping out of the Buckner House, I took a moment. Vic the Trader should be home, since Ardin didn't mention him being missing like in the game. From what I recalled, that was close to the beginning of the game, at around October 2241. I wasn't super sure about the date. 

The Duntons were closer though, and might have something a little more hands-on for me to do. Still, I didn't want to spend too much time with the two twin bullies. A brisk walk past the Bath-house, and I was at Vic's shack. By square footage, it was actually pretty big, though it seemed to be composed mainly of scrap wood and zinc plate.

A buzzing sensation in my mind alerted me to the two tokens I had won recently. No time like the present.


[**Rolling 1 star Novelty...**]

[**You have unlocked...**]

[[**Rank D Novelty: Tome of the Way of the White - Dark Souls**]]

*A large tome containing the scriptures of the Way of the White. Within the scriptures are numerous spells related to healing, protection, finding your way, and sending out blasts of force. It will take some study to learn them, but it is possible.*

*'Oh snap, a cleric build is now in session. Actually, what was I, some mutant bastard of Wyll and Shadowheart now?'*

[**Rolling 1 star Skill...**]

[**You have unlocked...**]

[[**Rank D Skill: Muay Thai**]]

*The art of Muay Thai is also known as the art of eight limbs, focusing on striking with the hands, elbows, knees, and feet. You've got a good grounding in the art, but becoming a true master will take practice.*

I felt the flow of the unfamiliar skills through my mind. How to throw fast strikes, to use the bones of my elbows, shins and knees to deliver club-like blows, and how to condition the self to use aggression in a controlled and focused manner. The supporting discipline would help a lot with Battle Maniac, or at least point it downrange safely.

Moving my body around a little helped to seat some of those techniques in, but while I was doing so, I noticed I had an unusual weight in my satchel bag. I opened it to find a little wooden doll. 

*'This must be my Totem of Undying. Heh, it's pretty cute'*

As if to mock me, a large white book dropped right at my feet out of the ether, with not even a flash to mark its arrival. I picked up the massive work and leaved through some of its contents. 

I wasn't expecting an indexed work with an ISBN, and really it didn't seem like I could just hop, skip and jump. Still, the first 10 or so pages seemed to make sense to my mind, mostly about Healing the body through channeling faith, and using faith as a omnidirectional kinetic push. I would need to read this before bed.

If I could find a bed. My empty wallet complained. I would need to somehow convince Vic or the Duntons.

Hefting the book under the crook of my arm, I knocked. * TAP TAP * 

A muffled "Coming!" from behind the door, and soon after a portly round-faced man wearing shirt and suspenders greeted me.

"Can I help you?"

I straightened my own clothes out. "Hi, I was hoping to talk to you about a possible opportunity?"

At this point I suddenly realized I came off like a Mormon missionary, which was extremely haram in my mind.

Vic looked at me oddly. "I'm not in the business of giving hand outs" he dismissed me as he made to close the door.

I stuck my foot in the crack. Super Heavyweight managed to give my foot enough mass to stop the door from dragging and tripping me. Vic grunted at the sudden resistance and looked at my foot.

"Sir," I said. "I know this is out of the blue, but really, you're the best person in town for me to approach. This has to do with *old memorabilia*." I had gone a little *sotto voce* with the last bit to try and inject some intrigue. "And I'm not bad at putting junk together, I suppose."

"Hnggh. Hmm. Okay. I don't really like being put to pressure like this, I need to get ready for a trip to the Den for merchandise." said Vic.

"Well, instead of hoofing it all the way there...why not use my car?"

"Sorry, did you say car?"
