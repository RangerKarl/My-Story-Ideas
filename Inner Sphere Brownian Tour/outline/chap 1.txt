Chapter 1.1 - Intercept

A mercenary lance, riding in a dropship are surprised when the dropship performs an unscheduled rendezvous. It turns out they have picked up the SI, one Karl Ibrahim

Karl knows he is in Battletech but isn't really aware of what era or where in the inner sphere he is. At this point he only knows that, so because of his gaslighting malus he accidentaly makes it look like he has some sort of oxygen deprivation.

Confined to guest quarters, he resigns himself to self-retrospection for a bit. He understands and is a little excited to be in the universe he's been reading and playing in for so long, but is a bit worried why he was ripped from his life.

The crew of the dropship are confused as to why there was a DropShuttle and a strange bunch of wreckage on the way to their JumpShip, as no one radioed in a misjump during their patrol.

The lance commander introduces himself to Karl and offers to escort him to Outreach, the up-and-coming hub for merc stuff. 

Maybe there will be work there.

Introductions to the rest of the Lance here would be nice too.

As Karl turns in for the night, his OmniGacha system properly kicks in and he starts to roll. 

His Vehicle rolls end up being inside a Gacha Warehouse, with proportional maintenance costs depending on vehicle condition.

It tells him the specific tweaks for this universe, and promises a Great Reward if the player achieves something noteworthy.
