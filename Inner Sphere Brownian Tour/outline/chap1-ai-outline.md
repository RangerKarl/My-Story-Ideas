# Chapter 1 — Morning Mirage

Outline based on the current manuscript in `manuscript/chap1.md`. The chapter follows the protagonist from his arrival through the fighting in Verdigris, contact with Dagger Lance, and discovery that he is on Enkra III in 3021. This outline supersedes the earlier DropShip pickup premise in `chap 1.txt`.

## 1. Somewhere, in the skies — Arrival and emergency landing

POV: unnamed protagonist, first person.

- Awaken inside an unfamiliar mecha cockpit, already falling toward the ground.
- OMNIGACHA announces enrollment, preset curses, and an attached Wiki Warrior setup that disables or redirects certain rolls.
- Establish the curses: Forgetful Franchise removes relevant plot knowledge; Gaslighting Isn't Real creates a compulsion to mislead people, with a month of resistance or a suitable cognition-reset roll offered as resolutions.
- Process the starting allocation: one 4-star roll, two 3-star rolls, and five 2-star rolls from curse selection.

| Starting roll | Result | Capabilities established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-star | Sutherland, Code Geass | Knightmare Frame with Float System, Factsphere, Landspinners, Blaze Luminous shield, Slash Harkens, assault rifle, anti-personnel machine gun, and stun tonfas. |
| 3-star, Wiki Warrior–Macross | Fire Bomber, Macross 7 | Ray Lovelock's piloting and management skills. |
| 3-star | Magpie's Eye | Highlights valuables in line of sight. |
| 2-star | Starjack, Rimworld | Space-adapted body with temperature and pressure protection, brief vacuum survival, and reduced muscle mass and strength. |
| 2-star | Apprentice Math | Mathematical knowledge and aptitude. |
| 2-star | Nimble | Increased reflexes and speed. |
| 2-star | Reservoir | Increased energy and recovery. |
| 2-star, Wiki Warrior–Macross | VF-19S Excalibur, Macross 7 | Advanced variable fighter capable of reaching orbit under most terrestrial gravity conditions. |

- Return attention to the immediate danger: an accelerating descent in approximately 0.8g gravity.
- Experiment with the controls, spread the Sutherland's limbs to increase drag, and locate the Float System through the stores display.
- Activate the Float System to slow the fall, choose a clearing, and survive a rough feet-first landing.
- Resolve to learn the Sutherland's capabilities; nickname it Suzie.

Scene purpose: introduce the protagonist through improvised survival and establish the system, starting assets, and liabilities.

## 2. At the clearing — Orientation, rewards, and a distress call

POV: protagonist, first person; continues directly after the landing.

- Find the Sutherland's tactical operations manual under the seat and open the cockpit.
- Take stock of a changed body: grey shirt and pants, hospital-style flip-flops, no familiar aches or gut, and less definition in the limbs. Set aside discomfort about the transformation.
- Spend roughly thirty minutes learning the controls, weapon selection, and operating limits, drawing on familiarity with combat flight simulators.
- Establish roughly a day's endurance if heavy shield and Float System use can be avoided.
- Attempt to summon the VF-19S to help with orientation; discover that claiming an M-sized vehicle requires laying claim to a hangar or hangar-like structure.
- Rise above the trees in the Sutherland and use the Factsphere to detect a cluster of unknown RF emitters.
- Receive three achievement tokens, then spend the two 1-star tokens:

| Achievement | Token | Result/status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survive a crash-landing in an unfamiliar vehicle | 1-star Skill | Quickening, from Bloodborne: short-range, difficult-to-track movement that severely strains the body. |
| Study and operate an unfamiliar vehicle | 2-star Vehicle | Unspent. |
| Find a lead | 1-star Generic | Agent 47 redirected by WikiTrawler into the SDF-2 Megaroad, from Macross. |

- Learn that the SDF-2 reward is a powered-down hulk somewhere in the system. Salvage requires locating it, performing a full startup, and assembling about 150 trained crew, including engineers and technicians; a training codex is somewhere aboard.
- React to the scale of the reward and the difficulty of finding a two-kilometer ship in a whole star system; worry about being pushed toward life as a space warlord.
- Fly at low altitude toward the signals while scanning communications, initially expecting local encryption to prevent eavesdropping.
- Receive an open distress call from Verdigris city hall: pirates are attacking, and the mercenary defenders are being overwhelmed.
- Decide to intervene, motivated in part by the prospect of further rewards and eventually claiming his own Macross.

Scene purpose: turn survival into action, introduce the vehicle-claim restriction and long-term salvage objective, and direct the protagonist into the raid.

## 3. Unknown City, Unknown Time — Relieving Crunchy's position

POV: protagonist, first person.

- Arrive after roughly twenty minutes of flight to find a burning town, wrecked combat vehicles, and a damaged spaceport. The original distress transmission has gone silent, but combat radio traffic continues.
- Contact an infantry commander offering to vouch for anyone willing to help. Receive the callsign Stranger 1, generally shortened to Stranger; the infantry commander identifies his position as Crunchy and marks it with green smoke.
- Use the streets and buildings to flank three wheeled, laser-armed APCs attacking Crunchy's position; switch from flight to Landspinners for the approach.
- React to an armed pirate raising his weapon by killing him with the Sutherland's 37mm coilgun. Earn a 1-star token for the first pirate kill and suppress the immediate reaction.
- Destroy one APC by firing through its open rear hatch, setting off an internal explosion.
- Maneuver around the remaining turrets and dismounted infantry, but dodge a grenade into an APC's firing arc. A center-mass laser hit forces an emergency shield response and triggers cockpit warnings.
- Fire the assault gun's underbarrel 105mm grenade launcher; discover that the loaded round produces smoke.
- Crunchy's defenders exploit the distraction with concentrated fire, including a field gun. One APC is damaged and the other reverses over its own infantry before colliding with it, immobilizing both.
- Use the Factsphere to track through the smoke, then combine Slash Harkens, cable retraction, and a Float-assisted leap to drive a stun tonfa through an APC's driver compartment.
- Cover the remaining vehicle with the assault gun and demand surrender. Surviving pirates comply; friendly infantry emerge to secure them.
- Recoil from the bloody aftermath while withdrawing the tonfa. Earn a 3-star token for defeating a vehicle platoon with assistance.

Scene purpose: establish the Sutherland's strengths and vulnerabilities in actual combat, give the protagonist his first kills, and earn local trust through a costly improvised rescue.

## 4. Crunchy's perspective — An unfamiliar ally

POV: Technical Sergeant Elias Timoshenko, third person.

- Identify Crunchy as the sergeant protecting the local school's faculty with about twenty soldiers, a secure basement, and an improvised strongpoint equipped with a field gun from an emergency warehouse.
- Recall an earlier attack by two enemy 'Mechs and the intervention of a mercenary light lance that drew them into combat. Subsequent stragglers pinned his defenders down again.
- Review Stranger's arrival from a local perspective: a machine smaller than a light 'Mech, gliding on wings and fighting with unfamiliar weapons and gadgets.
- Order the pirates secured and tell Stranger that he can stop covering the cleared Vargr APC.
- Observe the machine relax, stow its bloodied arm weapon, and manually free its harpoons.
- Hear Stranger admit this is his first fight. Offer to vouch for him with higher command and ask for help with the remaining fighting.
- Establish mutual willingness to make friends amid the raid.

Scene purpose: reveal the civilians protected by the intervention, show how strange the Sutherland appears to locals, and turn battlefield assistance into a personal connection.

## 5. Joining Dagger Lance — Rooftop attacks and a rout

POV: protagonist, first person.

- Receive an open side-channel frequency from Timoshenko and contact Dagger Lance with his endorsement.
- Dagger 1 accepts the referral and directs Stranger toward the fighting; he is not connected to their combat radio net.
- Use Slash Harkens and the Float System to clear the skyline. Ray Lovelock's breathing techniques help manage the acceleration, but the Starjack body's physical limitations remain apparent.
- Find four friendly 'Mechs fighting roughly six to eight enemies. Dagger 1 asks Stranger to draw fire so the lance can concentrate on individual targets.
- Check and reload the assault gun and grenade launcher before attacking from a building behind the enemy.
- Hit a desert-brown humanoid 'Mech in the rear with a grenade followed by a coilgun burst, damaging its internals and drawing return fire.
- Move to another rooftop and engage a bulbous, armless opponent. Frontal hits do less damage but maintain the distraction.
- A damaged, one-armed light 'Mech reaches the rooftop and fires missiles. Evade on Landspinners and empty the magazine into its weakened torso.
- Embed both Slash Harkens in the damaged armor, then combine retraction and Float thrust for a feet-first flying kick. Collapse the chest armor and disable the opponent, which falls from the roof and detonates below.
- Dagger Lance exploits the distraction, leaving new enemy wrecks while keeping its own machines in the fight. The remaining attackers begin to flee.
- Receive an invitation to the lance's FOB for resupply and introductions.

Scene purpose: escalate from an APC skirmish to BattleMech combat, show the protagonist supporting a coordinated force, and secure a place among the defenders.

## 6. After the fighting — Where and when

POV: protagonist, first person; continues in conversation with Dagger Lance.

- Respond to the invitation by asking where he is, explaining that he woke up recently and went straight into a firefight.
- Learn that this is Enkra III in the Aurigan Reach. The names unlock recognition: the rooftop opponent was a Wasp, another target was a Stinger, and Dagger 1 pilots a Centurion.
- Realize that he is in BattleTech. Receive a 1-star token for finding out where he is, followed by an objective to discover when.
- Ask the year and learn that it is 3021; Dagger 1 expresses concern and offers possible medical attention.
- Receive three more rewards: 1-star for discovering the year, 3-star for defeating a pirate 'Mech short battalion with assistance, and 1-star for making contact with Dagger Lance.
- End on the protagonist's weary realization that the immediate future will be busy.

Scene purpose: deliver the setting reveal through the protagonist's own discovery and close the battle with an invitation into the local community.

## Threads left open at the chapter ending

- The protagonist knows the setting and year, but his personal name remains unrevealed; locals know him as Stranger. FOB arrival, introductions, resupply, and any medical assessment have yet to occur.
- Dagger Lance's individual pilots are not formally introduced in this chapter. The current manuscript has no separate Mitchell patrol POV or advance warning of the DropShip's arrival.
- The immediate attackers have been driven off; the wider outcome of the raid and the extent of Verdigris's losses remain unconfirmed.
- The VF-19S remains unavailable until the protagonist claims a suitable hangar. The SDF-2 remains an unlocated, powered-down salvage objective requiring a trained crew.
- The Sutherland is the only vehicle deployed. Its laser-hit warnings, ammunition expenditure, and the pilot's physical strain make the offered resupply and assessment relevant.
- Quickening has been acquired but not used. Both curses remain unresolved; no explicit gaslighting-compulsion episode is shown.
- Unspent rewards shown in the chapter total five 1-star tokens, one 2-star Vehicle Token, and two 3-star tokens. The later combat and contact rewards do not specify token categories.
