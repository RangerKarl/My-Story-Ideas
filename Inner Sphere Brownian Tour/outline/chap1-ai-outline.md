# Chapter 1 — Morning Mirage

Outline based on the current manuscript in `manuscript/chap1.md`. The draft currently ends with Dagger Lance moving toward Verdigris; first contact and combat have not yet occurred. This outline supersedes the earlier DropShip pickup premise in `chap 1.txt`.

## 1. Somewhere, in the skies

POV: unnamed protagonist, first person.

- Awaken inside an unfamiliar mecha cockpit, already falling toward the ground.
- OMNIGACHA announces enrollment, preset curses, and the WikiTrawler modification that disables or redirects certain rolls.
- Establish the curses: Forgetful Franchise removes relevant plot knowledge; Gaslighting Isn't Real creates a compulsion to mislead people, with a month of resistance or a suitable cognition-reset roll offered as resolutions.
- Process the starting allocation: one 4-star roll, two 3-star rolls, and five 2-star rolls from curse selection.
- Receive the Sutherland, Fire Bomber piloting and musical skills, Magpie's Eye, Starjack body, Apprentice Math, Nimble, Reservoir, and F-36 Hornet.
- Return attention to the immediate danger: an accelerating descent in approximately 0.8g gravity.
- Experiment with the controls, spread the Sutherland's limbs to increase drag, and locate the Float System through the stores display.
- Activate the Float System to slow the fall, choose a clearing, and survive a rough feet-first landing.
- With the immediate crisis over, resolve to learn the Sutherland's capabilities; nickname it Suzie.

Scene purpose: introduce the protagonist through improvised survival and establish the system, starting assets, and liabilities.

## 2. Enkra, 3021 — Dagger Lance on patrol

POV: Leftenant Mitchell Devereaux, third person.

- Reveal the setting to the reader: Enkra III, a former mining world now supplying agricultural staples to the Aurigan Reach through Verdigris and its spaceport.
- Establish the security posting, arranged through the local lord and Aurigan Coalition government, and Mitchell's boredom after six months on-world.
- Introduce Dagger Lance through routine movement and banter:

| Callsign | Pilot | BattleMech | Introduction |

| Dagger 3 | Alicia Wilder | Jenner | Disciplined MechWarrior from Sian. |

- Arrive at Waypoint Bravo, a quiet lakeside position beside woodland and grassland.
- CP Alpha interrupts with a priority warning: a Pirate Point emergence has been detected, and a DropShip is approaching Verdigris on a course that will overfly the lance.
- Mitchell considers the danger of a nearby landing and the opportunity to approach the raiders from behind if they continue toward the city.
- Switch to laser communications, reduce emissions, and move into the trees.
- Detect searching emissions while the DropShip passes overhead; establish the area's limited air defenses.
- Plot a route back toward Verdigris and lead the lance through the forest using hand signals.

Scene purpose: establish the local cast and approaching raid while placing Dagger Lance near the protagonist's emerging search for other people.

## 3. Back at the clearing — Orientation and a lead

POV: protagonist, first person.

- Find the Sutherland's tactical operations manual under the seat.
- Take stock of the changed body: formerly a stocky Southeast Asian man approaching midlife, now pale, thin, reedy-voiced, and wearing hospital scrubs.
- Worry briefly about the family left behind and whether occupying this body has displaced someone else's son.
- Spend roughly thirty minutes learning the controls, weapon selection, and operating limits, drawing on familiarity with combat flight simulators.
- Establish roughly a day's endurance if heavy shield and Float System use can be avoided.
- Identify the immediate problem: no knowledge of the present location.
- Rise above the trees and use the Factsphere to detect a cluster of unknown RF emitters.
- Receive three achievement tokens, then spend the two 1-star tokens:

| Achievement | Token | Result/status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survive a crash-landing in an unfamiliar vehicle | 1-star Skill | Quickening, from Bloodborne. |
| Study and operate an unfamiliar vehicle | 2-star Vehicle | Unspent. |
| Find a lead | 1-star Generic | Agent 47 redirected by WikiTrawler into the K'ha'haf asteroid fighter. |

- Assess Quickening as a possible means of personal escape or defense, limited by short range and severe physical strain.
- Assess the K'ha'haf as a possible future space asset; it is space-only and lacks life support, weapons, and fuel.
- Wonder whether receiving both the Hornet and asteroid fighter points toward eventual space travel or combat.
- Set out at low altitude toward the lead, scanning radio frequencies and tagging patterned signals without expecting to decipher local encrypted communications.
- Reset the mission clock and commit to moving forward.

Scene purpose: turn survival into deliberate exploration, demonstrate achievement rewards and roll redirection, and start the protagonist moving toward the local situation.

## 4. Toward Verdigris — Mitchell wants his chance

POV: Mitchell, third person.

- Observe that the DropShip has left its reentry plasma shell and may soon be able to land or deploy near the city.
- Attempt to improve the picture with active sensors, without obtaining useful detail.
- Lilla questions the emissions and recommends continuing the advance.
- Reveal Mitchell's personal stake: his eagerness for a first legitimate kill outweighs his concern for civilians.
- End the current draft on his frustration at the possibility of arriving too late to participate.

Scene purpose: sustain the approaching threat and sharpen Mitchell's character before the two POV threads meet.
