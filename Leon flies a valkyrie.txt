From within the canopy of his VF-25, Captain Leon Sakaki, formerly of the SMS looked out at the unblinking sea of stars.

Fate had, of course, conspired without his consent. 8 years ago, he had been centre stage in perhaps the wildest experience of his career. 
A routine courier flight, out of Sephira into Ouroborous had lead him smack-dab in the middle of a conspiracy to change history itself. 

Fighting alongside his headstrong yet beautiful boss, and what he came to think as his adopted sister from the time of the Protoculture, he had faced down the many trials of the Fault-locked world. Working together with many legendary fliers across the universe, brought together by the time-bending capability of Yurwa Arga. 

Thinking back on it now, his career after that had slowed down a ton...

Aisha's flight plan, filed away in the data packet, was what seemed to be a slow guided tour. Because his loadout was assigned to him almost as soon as he touched down, just a day ago, he could surmise there was a long ballistic flight period. His route took him around 1 of the 5 artificial worlds that occupied Foegal-synchronized orbits, one marked on his Tactical Situation Page as Gusty. Gusty was a large, icy-rocky world roughly analogous to Earth or Sephira, except it should have been far more frozen tundra than snow resort given the planet's current place far away from the Goldilocks zone where all emigration fleet committees dreamed of occupying. 

ARIEL-III spat out the info packet on the planet through text-to-speech. "Foegal's most well known feature are the 4 Gravitational Lensing Arrays set up by the Protoculture, thousands of years ago. The arrays focus the white dwarf's latent photonic radiation into targeted beams sufficient to emulate stellar intensity of a higher order. The Bureau of Protoculture Heritage suspects the arrays contain other functions but this has not been corroborated."

Leon couldn't help but draw a mental line, between this ancient mystery and Aisha's presence in the system. She did a successful stint as a designer with Surya Aerospace here, after her squabble with S.M.S., using what could have legally been considered closed-source intellectual property. Of course she denied that the VF-31 project had very little to do with the YF-30 Khronos, but the lawyers disagreed. In the end, S.M.S. and LAI Corp took the YF-30 and sold it as their own take on the multifunction container system. 

Somehow, she must have discovered something here that tied back to the real reason the YF-30 was developed. An evolution away from the Variable Fighter as a tool of war, the Variable Dimensional Diver concept was meant to act as a probe for the multiple layers of fold-space, like a needle or syringe sticking into thick skin. 

"It was always the superdimensional fold dive system you cared about, wasn't it? What's in Foegal that has you revisiting the old idea again?"

The onboard computer rattled on as Leon occupied his time, scrolling through his stores management page. No long-range missile analogs on the pylons, just a high velocity GU-17C gunpod and Super packs, plus 2 drop tanks for reaction mass and fuel. The duel was supposed to be in close.
