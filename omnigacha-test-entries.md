VF-31A Kairos | Macross Delta
Rank C | Vehicle
Short desc: A 5th-gen Variable Fighter built by Brisingr's Surya Aerospace venture. This model was heavily used by Xaos during the Windermere conflict. Boasts a multimode container setup, allowing for rapid adaptation to changes in the field

A 5th generation competitor to LAI's VF-25 Messiah, the Kairos was a project planned by industrial concerns in the Brisingr star cluster to boost the local economy with an indigenous Variable Fighter design. With architecture inherited from its predecessor, the VF-30 Khronos, the Kairos shares the VF-30's Multi-mode Container system, allowing Kairoses to integrate multirole capability during atmospheric combat without needing to compromise stealth or aerodynamics. The standard Kairos loadout includes two 27mm rail machine guns mounted under the wings or under arms, the head turret, internal micromissile bays, and a dual-mode beam/mass driver gunpod that uses prefabricated ejectable emitter/magazines. A wireless electric charging projector is also available, to support Multi-drones in the field with power. The Kairos uses minimal Fold Quartz, opting for a newer design of ISC that saves a little more on cost. A little more prone to glitches than the Messiah due to its less-stringent quality assurance.

VF-25F Messiah | Macross Frontier
Rank C | Vehicle
Short desc: The VF-25F is a 5th Generation Variable Fighter built by Shinsei/LAI. This is the first production model VF to employ an Inertial Store Converter and the EX-Gear system, letting the pilot squeeze the Gs as he turns and burns. Comes in a fetching little red and white with black highlights scheme, but this can be changed.

A Low-Rate Initial Production example of the VF-25 Messiah, a type of Variable Fighter. This model was deployed during the Vajra War, as part of the elite flight team, Skull Squadron based out of Macross Quarter. The VF-25F is armed with a head beam gun turret, mecha-scaled combat knives, and a 6 barreled 58mm gatling gunpod that fires super-high velocity projectiles. It also has six underwing hardpoints for various stores, the capability of using Super and Armored Packs to increase effectiveness, and an Inertia Store Convertor allowing for limited nullification of G-loading effects on the pilot/plane. As with other Variable Fighters of the Era, it has a pinpoint barrier allowing a skilled pilot to defend against attacks.

VF-1 Valkyrie | Super Dimensional Fortress Macross
Rank C | Vehicle
Short Desc: The first production Variable Fighter and one to set the standard. Built to fight the Zentraedi in hand to hand as well as aerospace combat. Looks a bit like it's wearing bellbottoms in fighter mode. 

The VF-1 Valkyrie is a trimodal Variable Fighter craft capable of combat as a fighter, a robotic Battroid mode, or a halfway Gerwalk mode. Built out of ECA and advanced composite, the VF-1 carries a GU-11 55mm three-barrelled Gunpod capable of destroying Zentraedi mecha. Under its wings, it can load a host of NATO/UN Spacy rated payloads, from micromissile pods, to the RMS-4 Reaction Warhead missile. While not capable of independent surface to space exit without equipment, in atmosphere its endurance is practically infinite. A nice little camper car.

VF-0 Phoenix | Macross Zero
Rank C | Vehicle
Short Desc: Predecessor to the VF-1, the VF-0 was built as a demonstrator only to be pressed into emergency combat service due to reports of Anti-UN activity in the Mayan islands. Unlike other VFs, runs on kerosene/jet fuel. A little flimsier, but easier to maintain if you don't have antigravity on call.

The VF-0 is a hand-built demonstrator for the Variable Fighter concept, capable of trimodal transformation into a humanoid mecha, a fighter, or a halfway mode. The VF-0 is constructed out of ECA, but being powered by conventional internal combustion high bypass turbofans, it cannot provide the power needed to fully utilize them without extremely high fuel consumption. Made famous by a movie in the 2060s about the secret wars between the UN and Anti-UN, fighting against the then-unknown SV-51.

VF-19ADVANCE | Macross Frontier - Wings of Farewell
Rank C | Vehicle
The VF-19ADVANCE is a proposed service extension program for the VF-19EF Caliburn series of Variable Fighters. By tuning the integrated air combat software ARIEL to a finer standard, modifying the engines with firmware and intake upgrades, and using modern 2059-era materials, the VF-19 could be said to now stand toe to toe with the VF-25, albeit at the cost of lower endurance due to the lack of ISC. This model is armed with a GU-15 gunpod, and an adapted Super Pack from the VF-25 adding on more firepower in frontline engagements.

Fold Booster | Macross Plus
Rank B | Novelty
The Fold Booster is an externally mounted pod containing a Fold Engine. The Fold Engine allows any craft mounting it, that can interface with it via onboard avionics, to travel via superdimensional Space Fold, allowing for Faster-than-Light travel. The range of early examples was a mere 10Ly, but more modern examples can boast of a 50Ly range as well as zero-time fold, where time lag or lead incurred by FTL travel is eliminated.
