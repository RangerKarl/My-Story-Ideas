It was a fretful early morning, before the sun had even thought about rising. A nightmare had startled me out of my sleep, the details lost, but the feeling of an intense pressure to perform was still in my breast. Tapping my phone to check the clock, I saw the screen brighten to declare it was **3:13 a.m.** and I was probably not going to go back to sleep any time soon.

*Man, not the best time to lose my rhythm.*

As one does in the middle of the night without a plan, I fiddled with my phone looking to doomscroll on the usual channels. Thankfully, not 3 minutes before I started depressing myself into another fitful nap, I saw a thread.

"Try out the OmniGacha, for those looking to play with words."

*Well, I guess it can't hurt to try it out. *

Now, anyone would know that clicking random links would not be the best decision to make, but in my defense I wasn't exactly in the right frame of mind.

I tapped into the hyperlink, and it lead me to a charming little site with a picture of a gashapon dispenser and an introduction paragraph. 

"Says here... 1 4-star token and 2 (two) 3-Star tokens for an insert-type character. Huh. What's this about curses?". My finger kept on swiping. Turned out I could pick out a couple of curses just to spice things up.

"Well, ok. Let's try **Rolling** one and see what it gets me."

[[Curse: Battle Maniac]]
Description: You love fighting to the point that you will always seek out stronger and stronger opponents to fight to the death

Resolution: Acquire enough discipline to hold your battle fever in check, or increase mental resilience
Target: YOU
Affects: Combat
Reward: 2x 4 star

*Alright, 3 4-stars and 2 3-stars is a lot! Sounds really neat.* 

This already sounded pretty wild to me, in my head I was already drafting up silly fantasies about blowing up platoons of goons and amassing accolades. Calm down, fool! Had to roll first.

I clicked the funny gashapon machine, it had already loaded a 3-star. Fire away, then lads.

[Rolling...]

[**You have unlocked...**]

[**Rank D Ability: Sunbeam**]
Description: You fire beams that burn as hot as the Sun itself, bringing the power of sunlight with them. They also count as genuine sunlight, so anything strengthened or weakened by the Sun is affected accordingly.

*No idea whether that means it's just as hot as being under a midday Death Valley sun, or literally the surface of the sun. Oh well. Again!*


[Rolling...]

[**You have unlocked...**]

[**Rank D Passive: Super Heavyweight**]
Description: Your body is inexplicably heavier than it should be. Never in a way that causes actual issue, but you acts and move as if you have a weight many times what you should possess. Crack the floor with a heavy step or watch and laugh as grown men fail to pick you up.

*Okay okay, I need to lose a little weight. Chill. Alright, next rolls...*


[Rolling 4 star...]

[**You have unlocked...**]

[**Rank C Ability: Assembly**]
The hands remember. When you personally assemble something, you gain the memory of assembling it. From then on you may automatically assemble that item instantly provided you have the required parts. The parts don't need to be the same quality as what you originally used, but using substandard or better parts won't have the Assembled item compensate or make use of that. You'd have to build it by hand again.

[Rolling 4 star...]

[**Rank C Consumable: I O U**]
An I O U issued by the Gacha itself. Redeem it after a two months have passed to receive three random rolls of the same rank as this token. (\*\*\*\*)

[Rolling 4 star...]

[**Rank C Consumable: Totem of Undying (Minecraft)**]

As long as this item remains on your person, the next attack or effect that would kill you instead restores you to full health. The item is consumed after triggering. Restocks once per month.

I thought that sounded pretty promising. A highly stable firing platform for whatever a Sunbeam could entail, the ability to instantly reassemble something out of parts after building it once, a token to get 3 random 4-star rolls, and a thing that would guarantee I don't die once a month. Cool.

I felt the sudden urge to lay my head down. I turned my phone off, then put my head on the pillow again. Before I suspected a thing, I immediately drifted away...
