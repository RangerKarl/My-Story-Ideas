It was a fretful early morning, before the sun had even thought about rising. A nightmare had startled me out of my sleep, the details lost, but the feeling of an intense pressure to perform was still in my breast. Tapping my phone to check the clock, I saw the screen brighten to declare it was **3:13 a.m.** and I was probably not going to go back to sleep any time soon.

*Man, not the best time to lose my rhythm.*

As one does in the middle of the night without a plan, I fiddled with my phone looking to doomscroll on the usual channels. Thankfully, not 3 minutes before I started depressing myself into another fitful nap, I saw a thread.

"Try out the OmniGacha, for those looking to play with words."

*Well, I guess it can't hurt to try it out. *

Now, anyone would know that clicking random links would not be the best decision to make, but in my defense I wasn't exactly in the right frame of mind.

I tapped into the hyperlink, and it lead me to a charming little site with a picture of a gashapon dispenser and an introduction paragraph. 

"Says here... 1 4-star token and 2 (two) 3-Star tokens for an insert-type character. Huh. What's this about curses?". My finger kept on swiping. Turned out I could pick out a couple of curses just to spice things up.

"Well, ok. Let's try **Rolling** one and see what it gets me."

I woke up with the sun beating down on my face. I groaned, hand moving to cover my eyes as the warm rays bathed me, somehow ignoring the walls and ceiling of my room?

My eyes suddenly snapped open, to take in the view. I could only see sand around me for what seemed to be miles. I could see some mountain ranges in the distance, but otherwise, I had no clue where I was. Some sort of desert. 

*Sadly not a dessert. I could use breakfast*

I got to my feet, suddenly noticing I wasn't wearing my normal nightwear (sarong and tatty shirt). Instead I was wearing my work slacks, and a collar-free shirt. My 5 litre satchel bag was on me, albeit without any of my every-day carry stuff. My shoes were my slip-ons, meant for light walking, not... wherever i was.

I also had my gut on me. I had not taken very good care of myself recently, with only a medical note from my doctor telling me I needed to make some changes putting me on a decent track. Even then, regularly walking to work as well as the occasional run wasn't making much of a dent in it. 

*No point beating myself up for it. Having stores will be handy*

OK, point the first. 
[**Objective: Survive**]

A little *ting* in my head suddenly made itself heard. What was that??!

Off to my peripheral vision, a wireframe, or rather a *vector* display panel made itself known. On it was my Name, "Karl", some of my basic physical stats (height, weight), and what looked like *all the stuff I had rolled in the dark*

*Oh. I'm in one of those*

The desert was a lot of scrub, sand, rocks, and the lone tumbleweed. Well, I overstate the issue. I did see a bunch of cacti though, so at least there was something with H2O around. The problem was getting past all the thorns and stuff. 

Idly I picked up a small rock and chucked it at a stout, tubby little cactus. The rock made a dense *thkk* sound as it bounced off. It didn't sound too hollow, so maybe there was some water to be found.

Trying to scrape off all the needles with a rock could get annoying. I'd need some fine tooling, and maybe a bit of luck.

*Damn, maybe not the best option right now*

I sighed, and decided maybe another option was a better use of my time. Dejected, I started to walk in a random direction.

\***

The sun had moved a little bit. Without a way to tell time I wasn't too sure, truth be told, about what time it was. It was maybe late morning, assuming the sun worked the same way as the one back home. Judging by that same logic, I figured I was heading somewhat north.

Just then, I could hear something that sounded like growling. 

*Wait, what was that? Wild animals??*

I spun around. In front of me were 3 silver-grey lizards, the size of a small child each. Standing on their hind legs, and looking a lot like they were thinking *here's a fat little morsel for lunch*...

Adrenaline rushed into me. Imperceptibly, a rictus grin spread on my face. Instead of backing away, to the protests of the rational brain inside me, I instead squared up, balling up my fists.

***Well, I'm not feeling up to being an hors d'oeuvres!***

*Oh damnit, is that my curse coming up?! So this is how it feels to be of two minds, huh?*

The creatures, who looked rather like Geckoes from a certain old RPG I once played a ton of, immediately started to trot to me, jaws open and hissing. 

One of them, a slightly smaller beast, suddenly sprinted, and leapt! Its maw was wade open as it barrelled straight into me, hoping to snatch a bite from my belly. I hunkered down and put my forearms up to block, covering at least my eyes. 

The creature rewarded me by slamming into my arms. Instead of making me tumble down, though, the lizard immediately dropped limp. 

*What happened? Why did it not attack me further?*

My arms didn't come away unscathed, as they were somewhat gouged by the animal. I felt the cuts go deep into my skin, but it didn't feel like any bones were broken. I paid it back by giving its head a good hard stomp.

I was not expecting its head to crack like an egg. Its eyes almost bulged out, as blood flowed out of every orifice in rivulets. Its tongue, and indeed the rest of its body started spasming. 

*Oh, that's-*

First I needed to make sure they were dead. Two stomps to the twitching creatures' heads settled the issue, sprays of gore aside. I noticed that I had also set a couple of saguaro cacti minding their own business aflame.
